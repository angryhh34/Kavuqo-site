Kavuqo

Simple personal finance tracking, kept private.

Kavuqo is a local-first iPhone app for quickly recording expenses and income. Manual tracking stays free, while voice and typed Smart Entry make logging faster and Kavuqo Premium removes the Smart Entry usage limit.

Get support Privacy policy

Local-first

Your transaction data is stored on your iPhone. Kavuqo does not operate a backend for your financial records.

No ad SDKs or tracking

Kavuqo does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking.

Smart Entry

Voice, typed Smart Entry, Siri, and App Shortcuts can prepare transactions from natural language. Every Smart Entry still opens an editable confirmation before saving. Apple Intelligence, when available, is optional.

App Lock & export

Optional Face ID, Touch ID, or device-passcode App Lock protects in-app access, and CSV export lets you save or share your history through the native iOS share sheet.
